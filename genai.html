Generative AI (GenAI)

Artificial intelligence has increasingly become embedded in our technology and day to day lives. As an individual who regularly posts content to the internet, I believe it is important for me to disclose where I stand on AI, and how I use the technology when posting content.

AI Acknowledgement

I have used Generative AI to generate JavaScript code snippets in previous iterations of benjaminmacleod.com. Under the current release of this website, no Generative AI was used. Generative AI may be used for other websites under this domain— where required, additional declarations may be provided.

Why did I use AI?

I have a basic understanding of HTML and CSS. Where possible, I try to manage my own website and blog’s codebase. I made the decision to use AI as a tool so I could continue to write HTML/CSS (something I enjoy) without getting burnt out of the project. AI provided me the ability to write some of my own code instead of using Google Sites, which is not as customizable. For clarity, I did not ask AI to “Generate me a website”. This website took (and continues to take) many hours of my life to maintain.

When Generative AI was used perviously, I used a variety of AI programs (usually ChatGPT or Microsoft Copilot) to help generate Javascript snippets and debug issues with my CSS. Whenever GenAI was used for the development of this website, I manually reviewed all code snippets.

Another important note: I do not use tools like Codex. These tools require extraordinary system privilages, and I do not want to grant them to an agentic tool.

What about my other content?

I have written a detailed AI Use Policy that outlines how I use/plan to use AI for creating online content. Since AI is constantly changing, this document will be updated regularly. I encourage anyone with an online presence to consider developing their own version of this document.
