Storm Days in CCRCE

stormday.benjaminmacleod.com

The CCRCE Storm Day Statistics website is an unofficial collection of cancellations, early dismissals, delayed arrivals, and bussing restrictions from the Colchester region of the Chignecto Central Regional Centre for Education (CCRCE).

South Colchester Academy Archives

archives.scatv.ca

SCA Archives is a digital collection of yearbooks, video projects, and additional content. SCA Archives contains records from South Colchester Academy (2003 - Present), as well as the former South Colchester High School (1950 - 2003).

SCA Athletic Awards 2025 Video Series

I was commissioned by South Colchester Academy to create a series of videos for the school's annual Athletic Awards Banquet. The project entailed creating individual videos for each sport and were played before each team was called up to receive awards. I also created a slideshow of the school's athletes of the year and provincial banners, which was played on loop as families arrived to the event.

SCA Grad Video 2024

During my graduating year of high school, I was responsible for compiling the annual grad video at South Colchester Academy. This project required extensive planning to ensure that all graduates had the opportunity to be included in the video at least once. I also collaborated extensively with the school yearbook committee to capture photos of the graduating class of 2024 throughout the year.

SCA Spirit Video 2024

A friend and I planned and led a school wide 'lip dub' style video at South Colchester Academy. Each participating homeroom was responsible for choreographing their portion of the video. As Co-Director, I was directly responsible for the production aspects of the video, including arranging camera crews, video editing. I also worked closely with the other Co-Director to determine the film route and scheduled homeroom classes to specific regions of the building.
